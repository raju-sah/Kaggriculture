## One controlled experiment

Build a **workload and cash runway gate for third-land purchase** on a frozen copy of the current repaired agent. Keep crop layout, harvest thresholds, species targets, and executor priorities fixed. In `_bridge_market`, replace the `day >= 10`/cash threshold used by `agent_macro.py` with a state-derived admission test:

1. Estimate the next three days' mandatory cash for the existing livestock's feed, the daily hire ladder needed to service live feed/care plus harvest-deadline tasks, and seeds for the intended new strawberry cells. Assume **zero speculative sales** before the next crop maturity; credit only saleable inventory already in the shed at the current posted price and buyer availability.
2. Buy the third land only if, after its 2,000 cost and initial seed commitment, the cash runway covers those obligations plus a reserve. A first implementation should require at least 4,500 cash after land/initial seed orders; tune that number only on the spent first screen. The day-10 macro's 2,031 post-purchase cash would fail this gate, while the first joint policy's 7,867 cash at the end of day 13 suggests a later expansion may be fundable. This is a hypothesis, not a promised purchase day.
3. Include forecast worker action capacity: count urgent feed, care, ripe melon, and fresh-plant watering tasks plus travel from current hand positions, then compare with available hand turns over the same window. Admit land only when affordable same-day hires can cover the incremental plot work. Do not count empty plots as productive capacity. Record predicted versus observed cash, hired hands, missed harvest deadlines, and accepted sales for every trial.

The narrow code seam is `_bridge_market(s, stats, day)` plus a read-only workload summary prepared by the task planner before market orders. This is a state-based capital decision, not another fixed land day. It directly tests whether the third-land loss came from buying productive assets faster than the agent could fund and service them.

**Predeclared first screen:** spent seed 94700, both seats versus frozen V56 and frozen V70, with zero execution errors and exact replay retention. Against V56, require at least **85,962 own cash in each seat** (+15,000 over the current repaired agent's 70,962), **at least 110,000 accepted sales in each seat** (about +17,000 over its 92,873), no zero-sales streak longer than two days between days 10–16, and at least eight paid hands whenever three lands are active during that interval. All bought animals must be placed within 48 hours or the purchase blocked. Also require at least 2/2 wins against V70. If any condition fails, reject this variant rather than tuning on a new seed. If it passes, test spent 92802, then retain spent 92804 for confirmation under `README.md`. These are screening thresholds, not claims of 100% win rate or top-ten readiness; neither target is proven by the saved games.
