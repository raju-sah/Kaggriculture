# V74 original economic macro-template screen

## Result

The staged search stopped after one candidate narrowly exceeded the V70 own
cash on spent seed 94700, then fell below V70 on the second training seed.
The best seed-94700 template was **land10_crop_only**: it made $58,668 against
V56, a $1,602 lift over V70's $57,066. On spent seed 92802 it made $52,944,
$6,778 below V70's $59,722. It lost both seat orders on both seeds against
V56, had no execution errors, and did not meet the rule for spending
92804. There was no submission or fresh-seed run.

The crop-only template made fewer direct production actions than V70 and
earned only a small first-seed gain. On 92802, it had the same 1,386 direct
production actions as on 94700 but lost $6,778 relative to V70. This is not a
stable cash improvement. Several berry variants raised production actions
without raising own cash, which reinforces that action volume alone does not
measure accepted contribution. Direct production counts combine farmer and
hand PLANT, WATER, HARVEST, FERTILIZE, FEED, CARE, and
COLLECT_FERTILIZER actions. Cash is the primary metric; margin is reported
separately because a lower opponent score can otherwise look like progress.

The confirmation rule was at least **$15,000 more own cash than V70 on both
training seed blocks**, with no hidden candidate error. The only template that
beat V70 at all on 94700 missed that rule on 92802 by $21,778; 92804 remained
unused for confirmation.

## Template design, constraints, and provenance

There are eight original investment/production templates plus one V70 control.
They vary the requested day for a third quadrant, the size and price gate of a
strawberry expansion, or use crop-only expansion. All policies read the live
observation each turn and recompute tasks and routes; none contains a saved
turn-by-turn trajectory, seed lookup, or opponent code. The independent files
are generated by
[`experiments/v74_templates/generate_templates.py`](../experiments/v74_templates/generate_templates.py)
and their exact parameter/source hashes are in
[`manifest.json`](../experiments/v74_templates/manifest.json).

The sole source basis is the in-house, frozen V70 controller
`experiments/v70_capacity/agent.py`, SHA-256
`2002db9b4bbf20c654805adc35617eb787be3e9cb64e5aae46ae1044aad3b73e`. V74
templates are standalone snapshots in `experiments/v74_templates/agents/`; the
best seed-94700 source, `land10_crop_only.py`, is frozen at SHA-256
`41e8f42527fed2a7a4daec30e55de800daf0c553189b42e83fec28850ff7770f`. The
source contains no action arrays or external imports. All generated files
passed bytecode compilation, and the exact Kaggle environment loader ran each
template in paired seats without candidate errors.

At runtime, market decisions use live cash, current inputs, and the engine's
land, seed, and Fibonacci hire prices; expansion orders must leave the
controller's $1,000 operating reserve. Feed decisions use actual placed-animal
counts and current shed/inventory. Farmer and hand tasks are reconstructed
from board state and assigned using live positions and Manhattan route cost.
The engine's planting-day watering requirement, crop maturity/expiry windows,
and animal feeding/care obligations are retained. Thus unaffordable, blocked,
or too-late actions fall out of the live plan rather than being replayed as a
fixed route.

## Evaluation record and compute

The first stage used seed **94700**, both seat orders, and exact-loader
V56 comparisons for all nine entries (18 games). Only `land10_crop_only`
beat V70's own cash and was advanced to seed **92802**, both seats (two more
games). The source and report files are under
[`experiments/v74_templates/runs`](../experiments/v74_templates/runs/), with
stage summaries in `screen_results.json` and `staged_results.json`. Search
wall time was about **112 seconds** total, including the conditional
92802 expansion; match concurrency never exceeded four. Mean exact match
runtime was about **18 seconds**. All completed V56 games were losses for the
candidate, and all candidate statuses completed without errors.

The intended next step is not another adjustment to this same field-size or
third-land schedule. The cash-only template result should be combined with the
independent elite cashflow audit before another screen is designed; any later
search should alter a supported source of accepted contribution, not only
increase crop or labor activity. Keep 92804 and all final/validation seed
allocations sealed unless the stated two-block cash gate is met.
