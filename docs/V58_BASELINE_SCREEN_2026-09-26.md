# v58 baseline screen — 2026-09-26

## Outcome

The frozen v57 reset baseline did not meet the predeclared local readiness gate. It won 66 of 110 games (60.0%), with no failed or incomplete games. The separate requested 100% sweep also failed: only v54 and v56 reached 10/10 wins; the baseline lost at least one game to each of the other six historical agents. Do not treat this baseline as ready for the reserved final holdout.

The 95% exact binomial interval for the overall win rate is 50.2%–69.2%. Resampling the five whole seed blocks gives a 95% interval of 47.3%–80.0%; this is the primary uncertainty estimate because results on the same environment seed are clustered. Both intervals describe this local panel and do not establish a Kaggle rank or future win rate.

## Unlocked-shop evidence

Only three replay files were retained, all for seed 92902: v52, v53, and v54, candidate seat 0. They expose unlocks on days 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, and 24. The observed sequences and product demand per shop instance per tick were:

| v52, loss | Pizza, Pizza, Yarn, Bakery, Ice Cream, Smoothie, Pizza, Ice Cream | Milk 6, wheat 6, strawberry 3, tomato 3, egg 1, wool 1 |
| v53, loss | Pizza, Pizza, Yarn, Bakery, Ice Cream, Smoothie, Pizza, Ice Cream | Milk 6, wheat 6, strawberry 3, tomato 3, egg 1, wool 1 |
| v54, win | Pizza, Pizza, Yarn, Bakery, Ice Cream, Smoothie, Pet Cafe, Smoothie | Milk 5, wheat 4, strawberry 3, tomato 2, carrot 1, egg 1, wool 1 |

The two retained loss replays share the same shop sequence but have different normalized full-game replay hashes. The win replay has a different sequence. This supports prioritizing stress checks for milk, wheat, and tomato demand under repeated Pizza/Ice Cream shop unlocks; it does not isolate shops as the cause of the losses because the opponent behavior changes at the same time. No shop regime is inferred for matchups whose replay was not retained.

## Acceptance and next evidence

The requested historical criterion is 10/10 strict wins against **each** of v48 and v50–v56. It passed for 2/8 opponents and failed overall. The broader local gate requires at least 95% wins overall, at least 90% against every opponent and from every seat, a seed-block bootstrap lower bound of at least 90%, and zero execution errors. Only the error criterion passed.

This baseline screen used seeds 92900–92904. It did not use the reserved final candidate range 93000–93049, and no Kaggle submission was made. “Top ten” remains unverified: it needs current official leaderboard evidence for an authorized submission plus current head-to-head evidence against public top entries. The dated research snapshot in [V57 live research](V57_RESEARCH_2026-09-25.md) is background only, not evidence for this baseline or a future artifact.

Detailed machine-readable records are in `experiments/runs/v58_validation_baseline/` on the local workspace; the run directory is ignored by Git. The protocol and thresholds are in [V58 validation](V58_VALIDATION.md).
