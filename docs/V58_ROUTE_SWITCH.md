# v58 route-switch feasibility

**Decision: no-go; no paired score panel was run.** The only observation-conditioned gate tested was to retain frozen v57 reset unless a pinned route's exact immediate market cash exceeded reset at the first shop opening. That rule produced no route switches in its structural probe and offered no reason to expect higher future margins. The frozen reset route remains the internal reference.

The probe is [`experiments/v58_market_solver/route_switch/probe_openings.py`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/route_switch/probe_openings.py); its compact output is in [`opening_probes.json`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/route_switch/opening_probes.json). It queried the frozen reset, Kaito v13, and Ahmed v43 agents on the same observation after three passive days for seeds 95400–95402. Each source proposed different farmer actions from reset, and all three disagreed on market orders. The first visible shops were PET_CAFE, BAKERY, and YARN_STORE. Ahmed and reset had zero immediate market cash in those states; Kaito's seat-one proposal cost $2,718 in each. This establishes action divergence only. Passive farms are unlike a played route, and immediate cash ignores the future value of purchases, so the probe does not estimate scores or margins.

Prior full-route smoke results give no support for switching into either source: Ahmed trailed reset by $3,260 on seed 95000 and $964 on 95001; Kaito trailed by $12,748 on seed 94900. These small local comparisons are not rating evidence, but they leave no held-out signal that would justify another panel. No seed 95400+ games were run, no route-switch agent was built, and no actions were submitted or copied into the reset route.

Both source snapshots remain internal benchmarks. Kaito v13 is Apache-licensed code whose runtime uses complete replay medoids; Ahmed v43 credits additional contributors for action schedules and shop routing. The notebook licenses do not resolve rights in those third-party action traces. This feasibility check therefore treats the agents as black-box local references under the existing source notes in [`V58_OPEN_AGENT_SOURCES.md`](V58_OPEN_AGENT_SOURCES.md), [`V58_LICENSED_BASELINES.md`](V58_LICENSED_BASELINES.md), and [`V58_REPLAY_RIGHTS.md`](V58_REPLAY_RIGHTS.md). It does not create a submission candidate.
