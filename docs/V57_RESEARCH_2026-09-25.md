## Engine facts that correct earlier research claims

The installed first-party `kaggle-environments==1.32.7` source is the authoritative source for our local benchmark. Inspect `kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py` and `kaggriculture.json` in `.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/`; upstream [official engine source](https://github.com/Kaggle/kaggle-environments/blob/master/kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py) may change after the pinned version.

- Milk's glut-price function is **linear**, wool's quadratic, and fertilizer's linear. Wheat and eggs have logarithmic glut curves. Earlier research descriptions treating milk as quadratic were wrong. (`MARKET_PARAMS`, lines 42–50.)
- `PET_CAFE` consumes **CARROT only**, not milk or eggs. Egg demand comes from `BAKERY` and `BRUNCH_SPOT`; milk demand from `PIZZA_SHOP`, `ICE_CREAM_SHOP`, and `SMOOTHIE_SHOP`. (`SHOPS`, lines 103–111.)
- Fertilizer is excluded from town-center consumption. Its default price declines 0.2 per unit of net inventory surplus, reaching the $1 floor at approximately 495 units above baseline. The earlier statement that fertilizer “never collapses” is false under defaults. (`MARKET_PARAMS` and `TOWN_CENTER_PRODUCTS`.)
- Town-center demand defaults to one unit of every non-fertilizer product every **24 turns**, not every turn. Shops default to ticks every four turns; single-product shops consume two units per tick. Duplicates stack. (`kaggriculture.json`, `townCenterSellInterval`, `townShopSellInterval`.)
- Only cash is final reward; shed capacity defaults to 100 and overflow can be lost. Market orders beyond the default limit of ten are silently dropped. (`kaggriculture.json`, `reward`, `shedCapacity`, `maxMarketOrdersPerTurn`.)

## Research implications, not proven improvements

1. Value liquidation against the actual price curve and expected future shop demand; selling every resource immediately can destroy value in a temporary glut, while delaying too long risks overflow and stranded terminal goods.
2. Choose herd and crop expansion based on realized shop demand and observable opponent production. A copied fixed sequence cannot inherit the opponent's adaptive decisions on a different seed.
3. Preserve endgame retrieval of worker inventory and shed selling while avoiding worker routes that delay deposits until after the horizon.
4. Benchmark both seats on fresh paired seeds and distinguish selection seeds from untouched evaluation seeds. Ten wins against each of eight historical agents would establish only an 80-game local record, not a universal 100% win probability or a top-ten live rating.
