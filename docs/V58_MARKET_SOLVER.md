# v58 market queue optimizer result

**Decision: reject this branch for promotion.** The optimizer prevents the retained wheat overlay from liquidating feed stock in the day-five failure state, and it removed two no-wheat feed attempts in one fresh control replay. However, its small fresh screen lost nearly $1,920 in average coin margin against the frozen v57 reset route. No broader screen or submission was made.

The implementation lives in [`experiments/v58_market_solver/market_solver_agent.py`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/market_solver_agent.py), with [`build_standalone.py`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/build_standalone.py) producing the one-file [`market_solver_standalone.py`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/market_solver_standalone.py). The generated candidate bundles the frozen v57 reset route and uses copied market quote and commit rules from Kaggle Environments 1.32.7; it does not import the local engine. Its exact quote function matched the pinned engine for 108 probes across all nine products and 12 inventory levels. The frozen reset source hash is `4173479fe1f80d031d570d1ea23b694dfe86e1d162317a4c9c7df0058a40208e`; the screened standalone hash is `6850631769122937114de900a510fe92ff05b72945af1f59dbf6b4158d55c5ac`.

The overlay leaves farmer and hand actions intact, trims wheat sales against the day's unfed-animal reserve after planned pickups, enforces the ten-order market cap, and uses a pairwise order search scored by simulated cash. It uses the current shared inventory and prices, which already reflect earlier shop demand. The current market queue runs before that turn's town consumption, so future shop demand and the opponent's same-turn market orders are not forecast; the “exact” oracle is exact for this seat's submitted queue against the observed inventory, not the hidden simultaneous response.

On 7,448 retained market observations from 20 v57 proxy replays, it changed the market queue on 464 frames and changed zero farmer or hand actions. The copied engine oracle found a cumulative immediate-cash delta of **−$31,105** over the saved queues: only four frames gained immediate cash, while conservative wheat reservations suppressed sales on 460 frames. Optimizer median and p95 latency were **2.14 ms** and **15.56 ms** per market decision, respectively, below the one-second agent turn timeout. Details are in [`retained_oracle.json`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/retained_oracle.json).

The predeclared fresh futility screen used seeds **95300–95301**, both seats, and v50 as opponent. It compared the same 4 seed/seat games for the frozen reset route and solver:

| Candidate | Wins | Mean candidate coins | Mean candidate-minus-opponent margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen v57 reset | 2/4 | $122,449.75 | +$439.00 |
| Market solver | 2/4 | $121,515.75 | −$1,480.75 |

The solver's mean candidate score fell by **$934**. Its mean margin fell by **$1,919.75**; the paired margin changes were −$1,483 on seed 95300 and −$2,356.50 on seed 95301. It had no execution errors or timeouts. The underlying per-game results, logs, and replays are under [`experiments/v58_market_solver/screen2`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/screen2).

Feed and herd auditing found 333 FEED attempts per game. In seed 95301 AB, the frozen route had two no-wheat FEED attempts and the solver had none; all other seed/seat runs had none. Both variants had the same animal counts at day 5, 10, and 15: 6, 13, and 17. The separate spent-seed 93700 check also had 333 FEED attempts, no no-wheat failures, and the same day 5/10/15 herd counts for both versions, while the solver scored **$1,914 less** and lost **$2,102 of margin**. The logs and replays are under [`spent93700`](../experiments/v58_market_solver/spent93700).

On the retained wheat-starvation observation from seed 93700, the saved day-five action requested `SELL WHEAT 4` with six unfed animals and four post-pickup WHEAT. The reserve reduces that sale to zero, preventing the overlay from emptying the remaining feed stock. The stock is still short of all six animals' needs, so this rule prevents additional market depletion; it cannot guarantee that the farm already has enough wheat to feed every animal. The historical counterfactual is described in [`V58_WHEAT_FAILURE.md`](V58_WHEAT_FAILURE.md).

One initial tournament output is intentionally retained at `experiments/v58_market_solver/paired/solver`: Kaggle selected `_optimize` as the raw Python entry point because the first builder redefined the existing `agent` key without moving it to the end of the module. Those games returned configuration objects and are invalid. The builder now adds a unique final `_market_solver_entrypoint`; the corrected screen above used that fixed standalone hash. An attempted larger screen was stopped at the parent agent's request after this futility decision. This is internal research only; it does not alter the main agent, frozen reset route, or any submission artifact.
