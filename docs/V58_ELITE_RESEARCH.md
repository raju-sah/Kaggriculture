The strongest repeated signal is **early reinvestment followed by materially greater throughput**. Across these routes, the opening is around 4–5 hands, 2 cows, 3 sheep, 6–12 melon plots and roughly 9–12 wheat plots. By day 6–9, the sampled top teams have usually unlocked a second or third quadrant and hired 8–10 hands; by day 12, the observed leaders run 11–12 hands and often 3–4 quadrants. Product mix varies substantially: DSM pushes more wool, DECEM more cows/milk and geese/eggs, and Boey's sample is an extreme wheat/fertilizer route. This argues for a policy that responds to market price, shop demand and cash flow rather than copying one elite herd sequence.

## Executed-sale audit and correction

I replayed the recorded actions with `kaggle-environments==1.32.7` and instrumented successful per-unit market commits. The replay matched the public observation after **all 719 transitions**, with zero difference in either farm's money, shared market inventory, or market prices. These counts are accepted transactions, not submitted quantities. In Boey's episode, actual accepted sales totaled **5,386 units and $261,207 gross**: wheat 3,519 / $114,588, fertilizer 878 / $38,628, strawberry 224 / $38,394, wool 214 / $18,261, milk 183 / $24,041, eggs 172 / $8,955, carrots 124 / $3,821 and melon 72 / $14,519. This corrects any reading of earlier order totals as sales; 8,467 wheat units were requested but 3,519 accepted, and 4,385 fertilizer requested but 878 accepted.

The same audit on frozen `experiments/v57_broad/v57_reset_candidate.py` in a local match against the replay-derived Boey proxy counted **1,738 accepted units and $174,268 gross** (not its final net score). Sales were led by strawberry (252 / $60,260), milk (221 / $46,691), fertilizer (327 / $13,797), eggs (224 / $9,322) and wheat (426 / $11,565). This makes the throughput difference concrete but does **not** isolate policy quality: despite using the same numeric seed 511533100, the local match did not retain the public game's shop sequence. In this engine, the day-keyed RNG is consumed by weed spawning over empty tiles before the next shop draw; differences in occupied farm tiles shift later draws (`.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py`, lines 869–891). Public shops were BRUNCH_SPOT, YARN_STORE, SMOOTHIE_SHOP, BAKERY, PIZZA_SHOP, ICE_CREAM_SHOP, FARMERS_MARKET, SMOOTHIE_SHOP; local shops were SMOOTHIE_SHOP, FARMERS_MARKET, PET_CAFE, ICE_CREAM_SHOP, FARMERS_MARKET, ICE_CREAM_SHOP, SMOOTHIE_SHOP, PET_CAFE. The opponent also differs (public Vadim versus a replay-derived Boey proxy), and the high local milk revenue is consistent with this non-matched demand path. Treat the local trace as diagnostic, not a controlled head-to-head.

**Testable policy lever:** test a small, shop-conditioned wheat production and liquidation lane after early capitalisation, gated on observed shop demand and wheat's expected net margin relative to the shadow value of the worker/plot. Boey's accepted wheat volume alone contributed $114,588; fertilizer added $38,628. Compare accepted units and gross/net revenue per hand-turn against the strawberry/milk work displaced across multiple seeds and both seats. Respect the engine's 10 market-order-slot cap per turn; submitted order slots and accepted unit volume are distinct. The audit utility is [`replay_market_audit.py`](../experiments/v58_elite/replay_market_audit.py); compact totals, time series and the RNG caveat are in [`market_audit_summary_2026-09-26.json`](../experiments/v58_elite/market_audit_summary_2026-09-26.json).

## Benchmark recommendation

1. Keep the dated leaderboard CSV snapshot as the external target and refresh it before any live readiness statement. The current local v58 agent must not inherit the existing Rajusah rating.
2. Use full public episodes from multiple current top-ten submissions as strategy evidence: at minimum the Boey, DSM, DECEM, M & M & P & Q and Vadim episodes above. Compare full time-series of accepted sales, price, shed inventory, labor, land and cash, not just end coins or requested sell orders. Follow with a fixed-shop counterfactual or multi-seed product-mix experiment to separate production policy from RNG-driven shop demand.
3. Keep reconstructed agents as a smoke/regression panel only. Their replay-derived behavior and static or partial response cannot represent the opponent's live adaptive policy. The [V58 validation protocol](V58_VALIDATION.md) remains the local acceptance gate; no local panel can certify the Kaggle rating target.
4. For stronger top-ladder evidence, run the candidate through ordinary Kaggle matchmaking only when a submission is explicitly authorized, then report its own submission ID, rating, public episode count and dated rank. No submission was made for this research.
